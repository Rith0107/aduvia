Step Cards

A monthly review, exported as one collectible object. Aduvia's own metaphor — adugu + via, a path shaped one step at a time — becomes the hero art: a generative staircase of nodes where the filled count is the actual data, not an illustration standing in for it. Rarity isn't a skin — Bronze through Prism Step is read directly off that month's consistency score, so a card someone shares is proof of something real. Hover or focus a card for the foil tilt.

Production notes. Drafted at display size (268×375, the real 2.5:3.5 trading-card ratio); an exported share image would render this frame at 2–3× into a PNG sized for Instagram (1080×1512) with the tilt/hover state baked out. Typefaces are the native Apple system stack (SF Pro Display / SF Pro Text / SF Mono) per the brief; production would swap in Aduvia's existing Manrope/Inter pairing if brand consistency with the rest of the app matters more than the system-font read. Card backs, a full rarity-name set per species, and the actual monthly-data wiring are the natural next steps once a direction here is picked.
